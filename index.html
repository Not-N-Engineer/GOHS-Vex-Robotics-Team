This is the WIP website for the unofficial Great Oak High School Vex Robotics Team

Details

We will be attempting to participate in this competition hosted on December 20th.

This means that after 2/29 we will have 11 maximum meeting dates. For the first 3-4 meetings we will be focused on course material. For the last 5-6 we will be focused on the design, coding, and presenting of the robots. The last meeting dates should be for judging which team (if more than 1 exist) makes it to the actual competition. After the competition, if the team qualifies to move on, they will continue working on their robot with the help of any club member who would like to volunteer. The content of meetings during the second semester remains to be determined, although I suspect it will go like the first but with a competition hosted within the club.

The first meeting should probably start on 9/1, though, since officers should probably take a look at the course material first, and we can still advertise for students to come to the club before 9/29. Due to the tight schedule, the earliest we can start, the better.
